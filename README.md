# Coffee Mountain · Café de la Montaña

A small 3D routing puzzle: guide donkeys and mule trains along narrow mountain trails to bring coffee cherries from the farms to the Wet Mill and down to the town Co-op, across five days of harvest.

**Play:** https://williammorgansgrokbot.github.io/coffee-mountain/

- One standalone, offline `index.html` (Three.js r160, painted sprites and cut scenes, procedural marimba music)
- Works on desktop, iPad, and iPhone (touch: tap a donkey, then tap its stops; drag to orbit, pinch to zoom)
- Includes per-day story scenes and an animated How to Play screen
